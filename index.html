Omer Avci

PhD Student – University of Ottawa

Welcome

I am a first-year PhD student at the University of Ottawa, working on elliptic curves with Antonio Lei and Payman Eskandari.

I worked as a senior research assistant under the supervision of Payman Eskandari at the University of Winnipeg between September 2024 - July 2025.

My research lies in the area of algebraic number theory and mainly focuses on torsion groups of elliptic curves.

I am interested in rational points on curves, elliptic curves, torsion groups, modular curves, and Galois representations.

My master thesis, under the supervision of Ekin Özman, focuses on classifying torsion subgroups of rational elliptic curves over cyclotomic fields.

I have taken multiple analytical number theory classes from Cem Yalcın Yıldırım.

I enjoy solving olympiad number theory problems when I can get my hands on one of them :)

I completed my bachelor's degrees in Electrical & Electronics Engineering and Mathematics at Bogazici University in Summer 2021.
